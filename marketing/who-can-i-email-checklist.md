# "Who Can I Email?" — 506(b) Contact Screen

> For Raj, before sending the warm-network email (`warm-network-email-506b.md`).
> **NOT legal advice — confirm edge cases with counsel/WeFunder.**

506(b) lets you contact people you have a **pre-existing, substantive relationship** with — and
**only** those people. "Pre-existing" = you knew them *before* this raise. "Substantive" = you know
them well enough to have a sense of their financial sophistication or situation (not just a name in a
contact list). Run every name through this before adding them to the send list.

---

## ✅ Green light — almost certainly OK to email

- People you've **personally met or worked with** (colleagues, partners, customers, suppliers, advisors).
- **Friends and family** you actually know.
- Past investors, mentors, board/advisory contacts.
- Professional contacts where you've had **real two-way conversations** (not just a LinkedIn auto-connect).
- Anyone who has **invested with you before** or seriously discussed it.

## 🟡 Yellow light — only if a real relationship exists; otherwise skip

- LinkedIn connections — OK **only** if you've genuinely interacted (messaged, met, did business).
  A one-click connection with no conversation does **not** count.
- People you met once at an event — OK only if you exchanged enough to know them; a scanned badge doesn't.
- Old contacts you haven't spoken to in years — judgment call; if you couldn't describe who they are,
  treat as red.

## ❌ Red light — do NOT email about the raise (this is general solicitation)

- **Purchased, rented, or scraped email lists.**
- **Cold contacts** — anyone you don't actually know.
- Your website's **newsletter / "investor updates" signups** collected publicly (these are NOT
  pre-existing substantive relationships — the signup came *after/because of* the raise).
- Mass distribution: company-wide blasts, listservs, alumni-wide lists, group emails.
- Anyone you'd have to *introduce yourself* to.
- Posting the email text or the WeFunder link **anywhere public** (LinkedIn, X, forums, Reddit,
  the website). Public posting = general solicitation, full stop.

---

## How to send (mechanics matter too)

1. **Send privately** — individually, or **BCC** so recipients can't see it's a broad send and it
   doesn't read as a public blast.
2. Use the **private/unlisted WeFunder link**, never a publicly indexed page.
3. **Keep a record** of who you emailed and why they qualify (the relationship) — if questioned later,
   you want to show the list was relationship-based, not a broadcast.
4. **Personalize** where you can — reinforces that these are real relationships.
5. **Send once.** A light, single follow-up to a non-responder is fine; don't blast repeatedly.

## The one-line test

> *"If a regulator asked me to explain my relationship with this person, could I describe who they are
> and how I know them — without mentioning this raise?"*
> If **yes** → OK to email. If **no** → leave them off.
