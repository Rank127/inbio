## 2. High-priority questions (please confirm before outreach)

1. Confirm the offering is correctly configured as **506(b)** on WeFunder's backend, and confirm
   **Form D** filing timing (within 15 days of first sale) and who files.
2. Is this round **accredited-only**, or are we admitting **non-accredited sophisticated investors
   (≤35)**? If the latter, confirm the **Rule 502(b) disclosure obligations** are satisfied (and that
   WeFunder provides the required disclosure package).
3. Confirm **self-certification** of accreditation is acceptable for 506(b) (vs. 506(c)'s verification),

4. **Integration with a future public round.** We intend a later 506(c)/Reg CF raise. Please advise on
   **Rule 152 integration** so that a future general-solicitation round does **not** retroactively
   jeopardize this 506(b) round (general solicitation as a one-way door).

**B. General solicitation (the core 506(b) constraint)**
5. Confirm our outreach plan is compliant: email/LinkedIn **only to pre-existing, substantive
   relationships.** Please give us your working definition of "pre-existing and substantive."
6. **The publicly-collected email list** (site "investor updates" signups via `/api/subscribe`): we
   believe these are **NOT** pre-existing substantive relationships and must be **excluded** from the
   506(b) offer. Please confirm.
7. Confirm that **LinkedIn InMail / Premium prospecting to people outside Raj's network** would be
   prohibited general solicitation (we are treating it as off-limits).
8. **Residual public traces:** the prior soliciting `/invest` page and pitch deck were public before
   removal. Should we request **de-indexing / cache removal** (Google cache, Wayback) to limit any
   lingering general-solicitation footprint?
9. Confirm the **private WeFunder invite link** is adequately private (not publicly listed/indexed)

**C. Outreach content**
