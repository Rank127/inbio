**Type:** Video suggestion -- 2-minute screen recording over a process flow diagram, or a whiteboard explainer

```
Let me walk you through what happens inside an iNBIO fast pyrolysis reactor. It takes less than 2 seconds.

STEP 1: FEEDSTOCK PREP
Biomass (wood chips, crop stover, nut shells — almost any dry organic material) is dried and sized to optimize heat transfer.

STEP 2: RAPID HEATING
The prepared biomass enters our reactor and is heated to 500+ degrees Celsius in the absence of oxygen. This is not combustion — it is thermal decomposition.

STEP 3: PRODUCT SEPARATION
In under 2 seconds, the biomass breaks down into:
-- Vapors (condensed into bio-oil)
-- Non-condensable gases (syngas)
-- Solid residue (biochar)

STEP 4: ENERGY RECOVERY
Syngas is routed back to fuel the reactor's heating system, making the process energy-self-sufficient.

STEP 5: PRODUCT REFINEMENT
Bio-oil is collected for fuel upgrading. Biochar is cooled, tested, and certified.

The speed is critical. Fast pyrolysis maximizes bio-oil yield. Slow pyrolysis maximizes biochar. We optimize for the market mix our customers need.

### Post 18 (Wednesday) -- FAQ: What Is Reg CF and Why It Matters

**Type:** Text-only
**Best time to post:** Wednesday 8:00 AM ET

```
Several people have asked me: "What exactly is Reg CF? Is this legit?"

Great question. Let me break it down.

WHAT IS REG CF?
Regulation Crowdfunding (Reg CF) is an SEC-regulated framework that allows private companies to raise capital from the general public — both accredited and non-accredited investors.

It was created under the JOBS Act and updated in 2021 to allow raises of up to $5 million.

HOW IS IT DIFFERENT FROM KICKSTARTER?
