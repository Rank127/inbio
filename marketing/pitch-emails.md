## General Notes for All Emails

1. **Compliance:** This is a securities offering under Regulation Crowdfunding. Never state or imply guaranteed returns, specific revenue/profit projections, or that the investment is "safe." Always direct people to the official WeFunder offering page for full disclosures.

2. **Personalization:** Before sending any cold email, research the recipient. Reference a recent article they wrote, a company in their portfolio, or a specific reason your company fits their mandate. Generic emails get ignored.

3. **Attachments:** For angel groups and accelerators, attach a one-page executive summary or pitch deck (keep it under 10 slides). For press pitches, do not attach anything unless asked — link to the website instead.

4. **Timing:** Send emails Tuesday through Thursday, 8-10 AM in the recipient's time zone. Avoid Mondays, Fridays, and weekends.

5. **Subject lines:** Keep them under 60 characters when possible. Use specifics (company name, location, technology) rather than generic language.

6. **CAN-SPAM:** All marketing emails must include a physical mailing address and an unsubscribe mechanism. For one-to-one outreach (press pitches, angel applications), this is not required, but include your contact information.

7. **Track everything:** Use a simple spreadsheet to log every email sent, the date, the recipient, and the outcome (opened, replied, meeting booked, declined, no response). Follow up once. Move on if no response after the follow-up.
